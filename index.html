Before you begin

Still have a question?

Contact the team
How do I start using Deviny?

Deviny is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Once it launches, download the app and create an account. You must be at least 16.

Do I have to choose a program or a specialist?

No. You can train independently, find partners and talk with other people. A specialist’s program is an option when you want a ready-made plan.

Can I get personal support from a specialist?

At launch, you can talk with specialists. Paid personal support and paid consultations will not be available.

Who can see my results?

You choose which results to share and who can see them. People can find you in GymBro only when you turn on visibility.
