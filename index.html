Still have a question?

Contact the team
How do I start using Deviny?

Deviny will be available in App Store and Google Play soon. After release, download the app and create an account.

Do I have to choose a program or a specialist?

No. You can train independently, find partners and talk with other people. A specialist’s program is an option when you want a ready-made plan.

Does a program include personal support?

This depends on the offer you choose. Check what is included and whether personal support or consultations are part of it.

Who can see my results?

You choose which results to share and who can see them. You appear in GymBro discovery only after enabling visibility.
